Purpose

A real scoreboard, not a tally app that outgrew its welcome

Most pool apps pick one job: a bare "+1" counter, or a heavyweight platform built for a tournament director running eight tables at once. Pool Master Counter sits in the gap between them — live scoring good enough to run from across the room, plus the stats, ratings, and bracket play a long‑running group actually wants, without ever asking anyone to make an account.

Not this

A stopwatch with two names on it

Race‑to counting and nothing else — close the tab and the whole night is gone. Fine for one game; useless for a group that plays every week.

Pool Master Counter

Score tonight, remember every night

The live board, a running rating for everyone, head‑to‑head records, and a basic bracket — free, local, permanent — for one table and the people who keep showing up to it.

Not this either

Tournament‑operations software

Built for 64‑bracket events across a dozen physical tables, a director approving results, an account for every player. Overkill for a Tuesday night.

The full tour

Everything on the table

Every screen below is the real app, not a mockup.

Live play

A scoreboard built to be read from across the room

Tap +/− on a player's card to track balls, points, or racks toward the target — each tap plays its own synthesized tone. Reaching the target banks the win and starts the next game automatically.

  • Focus Mode hides every setup panel down to just the board.
  • Milestone, On‑the‑Hill, and Game Changed pop‑ups mark a win, a one‑win warning, and a rotation switch.
  • Undo Last Win, Share Standings, Export Session — one tap away, always.
Look & feel

Nineteen themes, switched in one tap

A deep felt green through to a soft pastel shimmer, plus two genuine light modes and two high‑contrast pairs — every color that sits on an accent background is computed per theme to stay legible, so a button is never guessing at its own contrast.

  • Crimson Felt is the original look and the default.
  • Daybreak Chalk and Pearl Lounge are real light themes, not a lightened dark one.
  • Blackout Contrast and Paper Contrast push legibility as far as it goes.
Players

A roster that remembers itself

Add a name and it's auto‑capitalized and checked for duplicates as you type. Tap Standby/Playing to move someone in or out of tonight's game without losing them from the roster.

  • Saved player lists auto‑save every distinct group you've ever played with — one tap to load.
  • Names are case‑insensitive — "bob" and "Bob" are always the same person.
  • Removing someone from tonight never touches their career stats.
Tournaments

A real bracket, not a spreadsheet

Single elimination, double elimination, round robin, or Swiss, set up from the same wizard or the Tournament page. Brackets render as a connected tree — winners side, losers side, and a Grand Final that tracks a run all the way from the losers bracket.

  • Eliminated players show struck‑through, right in the bracket.
  • Drop to the losers bracket and you're not done — win it out and you meet the winners' champion in the Grand Final.
  • Every rack still counts toward career stats and ratings — it's not a separate pool of data.
All Players

Every player who's ever picked up a cue here

Sort by win percentage, wins, or name, and filter by period — today, a week, a year, all time. Switch to a cumulative line graph per player, with click‑to‑reveal detail on every data point.

  • Bar view: games played/won/lost, plus a timeline of when.
  • Graph view: tap any dot for the opponents and results behind it.
  • A small rating badge follows every name, everywhere it appears.
One player, in full

Every player gets their own page

Tap any name to open it: a stats synopsis for today through all‑time, a complete head‑to‑head record against every opponent faced, and the same graphs scoped to just them.

  • A header dropdown jumps straight to any other player — no trip back to All Players.
  • Session History expands into every individual game played.
  • Export Stats or Reset just this player's saved history, independently of everyone else's.
The night, written down

Notes tonight, a report you can actually send

A free‑text box for anything worth remembering — who's on fire, funny moments — saves automatically as you type. Copy, Email, or Text Report turns it into a real recap: every player's win/loss and rating move, total games, and your notes.

  • Built fresh from live and saved sessions, so it's accurate even after a mid‑day reset.
  • Ready to paste, email, or send as a text, as‑is.
Your data, your device

Backup, iCloud sync, and a real desktop app

Everything lives in the browser on the device it's used on — there's no cloud account. Export All Data for a full JSON backup, or Export for iCloud Sync to move a session between your own devices.

  • Import merges — nothing already here gets overwritten or double‑counted.
  • A free desktop download runs the local relay a second phone can join to score the same live game together.
Never stuck

One guide, contextual to wherever you are

A single Help & Guide overlay covers every feature on every page — and it's contextual: opening it jumps straight to the section for whatever page you're currently on, with the rest one scroll away.

Bragging rights

The Hall of Fame leaderboard

An MVP score blends win rate, tournament wins, rating, dominant wins, and long runs into one ranking, once a player has enough games on the board to be judged fairly.

  • Gold, silver, and bronze podium spots, with a plain‑language "why am I ranked here" explainer for every player.
  • A dedicated Teams leaderboard sits one tap away for group play.
  • Share the whole board as a report, same as any other stats page.
More in the rack

The rest of what's in here

Smaller in screen space, not in usefulness.

🔠

Fargo‑style ratings

Every player gets an automatic, Elo‑style rating on the same published scale FargoRate uses — a 100‑point gap works out to about a 2:1 favorite. No way to edit one by hand; it only moves from real games.

⚖️

Fair Match handicapping

Turn it on and the lower‑rated side gets a shorter race target instead of the same one — calculated to land the match close to a coin‑flip based on the actual rating gap.

🦦

Skunk detection

A loser who potted nothing gets flagged automatically for balls/points games, and derived from "balls left on the table" for the 8‑Ball family — correctly left out of 9‑Ball, where it isn't a real concept.

🎵

Sound with nothing to download

Every tone is synthesized live with the Web Audio API. Reach a race‑to‑N milestone and it plays the full 30‑note theme from Beethoven's 9th, pitched low with a reverb tail.

📱

Group Session

Host a session on the local WiFi and a second phone can scan a QR code to join and actively score the same live game — no internet, no account, nothing leaves the room.

⏳

Quick Counter

"Just give me a counter" skips the wizard entirely — a bare, editable tally with no game type, no target, and nothing written to storage. Reload the page and it's gone, on purpose.

🔒

Nothing leaves the device

No account, no server, no analytics. Every player, stat, rating, and bracket lives in local storage on the device you're using, until you choose to export it.
