🔢 15 Ball Rotation

Also known classically as "Rotation" or "61" — a race to score points rather than to pocket a fixed group of balls.

The rack

All 15 object balls are racked in a triangle: the 1-ball on the foot spot at the apex, the 15-ball in the center of the rack, and the rest placed randomly around them.

Shot order

On every shot, the cue ball's first contact must be with the lowest-numbered object ball still on the table. Failing to make that contact first is a foul and gives the incoming player ball in hand. Beyond that requirement, any ball may legally be pocketed on a shot — the shooter isn't required to call which ball goes in, only to contact the lowest ball first.

Scoring

Each legally pocketed ball scores points equal to its own number — sinking the 9-ball scores 9 points, the 13-ball scores 13, and so on. A player keeps their turn as long as they keep legally pocketing a ball; the turn passes to the opponent on a miss or a foul.

Winning

All 15 balls together total 120 points (1 through 15 summed). The first player to reach 61 points — half the total plus one — wins the rack outright, since the opponent can no longer catch up even by running every remaining ball. This app tracks each player's running point total directly on their card, and converts the balls left on the table into a win-odds estimate the same way it does for Straight Pool.

🎱 8 Ball Rotation

A house variant that plays like standard call-shot 8-Ball, but borrows Rotation's shot-order discipline. If your table plays a different house version, this section lives in its own file (rules/rotation.html) — feel free to edit it to match.

Object balls and groups

Break and object-ball assignment follow standard 8-Ball: whoever legally pockets a ball from a group (solids or stripes) first is assigned that group for the rest of the rack.

Shot order

Unlike regular 8-Ball, a shooter can't freely choose any ball from their own group — each shot must first contact the lowest-numbered ball still on the table (from either group) before touching any other ball, the same discipline 15 Ball Rotation uses. A wrong-ball-first contact is a foul and gives the incoming player ball in hand.

Winning

Once a player has legally pocketed every ball in their group, they must call their shot on the 8-ball and pocket it to win the rack. A foul on the 8-ball (a wrong-ball-first contact, a scratch, etc.) loses the rack outright for the shooter. This app scores 8 Ball Rotation the same way as regular 8-Ball — one rack won counts as one point toward the session's target.
