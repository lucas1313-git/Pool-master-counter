Video 2: Setting Up Camera Recognition
======================================

[0:00 - 0:12]
Pool Master Counter can tell who's shooting using a camera, so nobody has to tap the keypad mid game. Here's how to set it up.

[0:12 - 0:18]
Open the menu, and click Set Up Camera.

[0:18 - 0:28]
You can use a separate phone mounted over the table, or this tablet's own camera. We'll use this tablet's camera.

[0:28 - 0:35]
A short wizard walks through permission, calibration, and enrollment. Click Next.

[0:35 - 0:41]
Camera access is already granted, and shown here as active.

[0:41 - 0:49]
Calibration measures your real table, covered in its own video. We'll skip it here.

[0:49 - 0:58]
Enrolling teaches the camera what each player looks like, also its own video. We'll start playing instead.

[0:58 - 1:10]
Last, a few mounting tips: keep it powered, keep the screen awake, and aim it at players approaching the table, not just the felt.

[1:10 - 1:16]
Click Finish Setup to land on the live Recognize view.

[1:16 - 1:23]
This view shows the live camera feed, and who it currently sees.

[1:23 - 1:30]
That's camera setup. Next, the videos on table calibration and player enrollment.
