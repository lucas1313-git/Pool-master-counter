Video 1: Getting Started (Setup Wizard)
=======================================

[0:00 - 0:08]
Welcome to Pool Master Counter. This guide covers the basics: adding players, and starting a game.

[0:08 - 0:17]
The first time you open the app, a short welcome wizard appears. Click Go to begin.

[0:17 - 0:23]
Type your name. This becomes your first player on the roster.

[0:23 - 0:31]
We'll run the full Setup Wizard, which walks through every option step by step.

[0:31 - 0:39]
Step one picks the game. We'll keep Eight Ball and casual Individual play. Click Next.

[0:39 - 0:54]
Step two is where you add players. Type a name, click Add, and repeat for everyone playing today.

[0:54 - 1:06]
Step three asks who is actually playing right now. Switch each player from Standby to Playing, then click Next.

[1:06 - 1:17]
Step four offers an automatic game-type rotation. We don't need that today, so we'll leave it on No, and click Next.

[1:17 - 1:26]
Step five shows a summary of everything you picked. Hit Start Game to jump into the scoreboard.

[1:26 - 1:38]
And that's it - the scoreboard is live. Tap the plus button under a player's score to record a rack win for them.

[1:38 - 1:47]
That covers the basics. For camera-based player recognition, table calibration, and player enrollment, check the other how-to videos.
