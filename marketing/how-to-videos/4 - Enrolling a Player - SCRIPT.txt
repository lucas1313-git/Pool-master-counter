Video 4: Enrolling a Player
===========================

[0:00 - 0:10]
Enrollment teaches the camera what a player looks like today - their outfit, not their face. It only lasts one session.

[0:10 - 0:17]
Pick a player from this list to start enrolling them.

[0:17 - 0:30]
Then have them bend over and take a real shot at the table. Each shot captures a sample automatically - a beep, no button to press.

[0:30 - 0:43]
You don't have to enroll everyone up front. Scoring a real shot on the tablet also labels the camera's current guess, so normal play keeps teaching it.

[0:43 - 0:51]
Here's Riley, already enrolled today with several samples from different sides of the table.
