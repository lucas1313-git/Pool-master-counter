Video 5: Reading the Scorecard Camera Icons
===========================================

[0:00 - 0:09]
While playing, a small camera icon can appear above a player's name. Here's what each state means.

[0:09 - 0:16]
A flashing icon means the camera currently sees this player, standing near the table.

[0:16 - 0:23]
The moment that player takes their shot, the icon stops flashing.

[0:23 - 0:30]
If a new sample gets added to their profile, a small plus sign appears.

[0:30 - 0:40]
If the camera isn't sure who's shooting, a question mark appears. Tap that player on the keypad to confirm.

[0:40 - 0:49]
And while a player sits waiting their turn, a small clock icon shows they're still being tracked.

[0:49 - 1:00]
Flashing, a plus, a question mark, or a clock - four small icons that keep you posted at a glance.
