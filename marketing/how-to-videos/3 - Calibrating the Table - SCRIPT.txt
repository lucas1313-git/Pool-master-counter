Video 3: Calibrating the Table
==============================

[0:00 - 0:09]
Calibration teaches the camera your table's real size, so close-enough-to-shoot becomes a real measurement, not a guess.

[0:09 - 0:16]
First, pick your table size from this list. We'll use the standard eight-foot table.

[0:16 - 0:25]
You can try automatic detection first. If it can't find your rails, tap the corners yourself.

[0:25 - 0:31]
Click 'Calibrate manually instead' to switch to corner tapping.

[0:31 - 0:39]
Your first two taps trace one edge. We'll use the short end, which is already selected.

[0:39 - 0:46]
Click Start manual calibration.

[0:46 - 0:53]
Now tap corner one of the table, on the live camera view.

[0:53 - 0:58]
Tap corner two, going around the table.

[0:58 - 1:04]
Then corner three.

[1:04 - 1:10]
And corner four, to close the loop.

[1:10 - 1:17]
And the table is calibrated. Distance checks are now accurate for this mounting.

[1:17 - 1:25]
That's table calibration. You only need to do it once per camera position.
