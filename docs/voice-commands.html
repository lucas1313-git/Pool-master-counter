🎱 Pool Master Counter — Voice commands & Siri

Say it instead of tapping it · English · October 25, 2026

How to talk to the scoreboard

Hold to talk. Hold the 🎤 Talk button (score section, top row) — or hold the . key on the number pad — say one sentence, then let go. A quick tap on 🎤 Talk shows all the sentences.
What you see and hear. A big line at the bottom shows what was heard; a message at the top says what was done. Points beep like the number pad; questions (Q-score, the score) are answered out loud.
Names. Anyone on the scoreboard or in the Contact Sheet — nicknames work too. A name that sounds alike is found (“Luke” for Luc); when two names are too close, it asks instead of guessing.
First time. The browser asks to use the microphone — allow it. Chrome needs the internet (it sends the sound to Google to turn it into words). English only for now.

The sentences

Luigi, Luc, Bob and Chris stand for your players' names; numbers and games are examples. Say one sentence at a time.

Game and race

Picks the game being played now (this turns the rotation off). "Race to" is the number of games to win; "target" is the points or racks within one game.

  • “Play 8-ball”
  • “Now playing straight pool”
  • “Play 9-ball race to 5”
  • “Race to 7”
  • “Target 100 points”

Rotation

The games played in turn - say them in order.

  • “Rotation 8-ball and 9-ball”
  • “Add 10-ball to the rotation”
  • “Remove 9-ball from the rotation”
  • “Change game every 2 games”
  • “Rotation on”
  • “Single game”
  • “Clear the rotation”

Players

Anyone on the scoreboard or in the Contact Sheet - a new name is added. "Plays against" seats exactly those two and puts everyone else on standby.

  • “Add Luigi to the session”
  • “Luigi is playing”
  • “Luigi is standby”
  • “Luigi plays against Luc”

Teams

Seats them as Team A and Team B; everyone else goes on standby.

  • “Luigi and Luc against Bob and Chris”
  • “Luigi and Luc are team A against Bob and Chris as team B”

Scoring

Points games: the beep confirms, as on the number pad; without a name, the point goes to the selected player. Rack games: say who won - the balls left, "Nice game" and "Undo" work while the win window is open.

  • “Add one point to Luc”
  • “Add 3 points to Luigi”
  • “Take one point from Luc”
  • “Add one point”
  • “Luigi wins the game with 5 balls left on the table”
  • “5 balls left”
  • “Nice game”
  • “Undo”
  • “Yes / No”

Whole session

One sentence, every part optional, in any order: the race, the rotation's games and how often it changes, the players (from the Contact Sheet - nicknames are fine), and who's playing (everyone else on standby). Anyone not named leaves the session - or, with games recorded this session, goes on standby.

  • “Set session, race to 5, rotate every 2, players are Luigi, Luc, Bob and Chris, Luigi and Luc are playing”
  • “Set session, race to 7, rotation 8-ball and 9-ball, players are Luigi and Luc”

Recording

With the recording page open (🎬 Record, in this browser): Mark starts a cue a little before now (20 s, set on that page), Finish ends it - see 🎞️ Cues.

  • “Mark”
  • “Finish”

Questions

The answer is spoken out loud.

  • “What is Luigi's Q-score today?”
  • “What's the score?”
  • “Help”

Siri — “Hey Siri, Scoreboard”

With a Siri Shortcut you can say any of these sentences without touching anything — from your iPhone, iPad or the Mac. Siri listens, sends the sentence to the scoreboard on your Mac, and says what was done (“Luc plus 1, now 13”).

Make the Shortcut once

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, and name the shortcut Scoreboard (the name is what you'll say to Siri).
  2. Add the action Dictate Text. Set its Language to English (Stop Listening: After Pause).
  3. Add Get Contents of URL. URL: http://10.0.0.52:4173/voice. Tap Show More: Method POST, Request Body JSON, then Add new field → Text: key text, value: the Dictated Text variable.
  4. Add Speak Text — it speaks the Contents of URL (the scoreboard's answer).

Use it

Say “Hey Siri, Scoreboard”, wait for Siri to listen, then say your sentence — for example “add one point to Luc”, “mark” or “set session, race to 5, players are Luigi, Luc, Bob and Chris, Luigi and Luc are playing”.

If it doesn't work

“The scoreboard isn't open”Open the scoreboard on the Mac (reload it once after an update). It must stay open — in the background is fine.
Nothing happens on the iPhoneThe phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac. The first time, iOS asks to let Shortcuts use the local network — allow it.
The address changedThe Mac's address can change (a new router, a different Wi-Fi). The scoreboard's sentence list (a tap on 🎤 Talk) ends with a Siri section showing the current one — update step 3.
Two scoreboards openThe one used last (on screen) does the sentence — never both, so a point is never counted twice.
A wrong nameSay the full name, or the nickname from the Contact Sheet; the big line at the bottom of the scoreboard shows what was heard.

Recording: marking the best moments

With the recording page open (🎬 Record in the scoreboard's header), say “mark” right after a good shot: the cue starts 20 seconds earlier — the beginning of the shot — and is named for the player and where the race stood then (e.g. Luigi-8-Ball-game 5 of raceto 7-1m35s-…-cued). Say “finish” to end it. On the recording page's 🎞️ Cues panel (or the Cues page) every cue can be watched, its start and end set by hand, and exported as a clip — even while it records.
