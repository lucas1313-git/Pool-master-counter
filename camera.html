Welcome

Step 1 of 5

This page turns a phone mounted over the pool table into a player-recognition camera. It watches for players approaching and bent over shooting, and tells the scoreboard tablet who's up. Takes a few minutes to set up - camera permission, pointing it at the table, and enrolling players.

Grant camera access, and pick the right camera if this device has more than one.

Camera: starting...

Calibrating the table gives real measurements, so "close enough to be shooting" is an actual distance from the rails instead of a guess - and it tracks where at the table each player stands, for better accuracy.

Not calibrated yet.

Two ways to teach it your players: on the Enroll tab, press Shooting beside a player's name while they're bent over a shot - every press adds to what the camera knows about them, as often as you like, even mid-game - or just start playing: scoring a real shot on the tablet teaches the camera too. Either way, it remembers each player from day to day - only their shirt is learned afresh each new day, the same way.

A few things that matter once this is left running unattended:

  • Power - plug it in. Camera + detection drains the battery fast.
  • Keep it awake and foregrounded - turn off Auto-Lock (Settings → Display & Brightness), or use Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility) so a stray tap can't back out of this page.
  • Framing - aim for a view of players approaching the table, not just the table itself. Check the debug overlay (Settings panel) while positioning it.
